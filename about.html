The people behind the bakes

Our Bakery Story

Good baking brings people together.

North Star Bakery was built around a simple idea: make comforting, thoughtfully prepared baked goods and give neighbors a welcoming place to connect. From weekday breakfast stops to cakes for family milestones, we are proud to be part of the moments that make a community feel like home.

Thoughtful ingredients

How we source

We aim to choose quality ingredients and work with dependable suppliers. When seasonal or local ingredients are available and suit a recipe, we look for ways to use them. Our goal is to bake responsibly while keeping flavor, freshness, and consistency at the center of every batch.

Made in small batches

We value careful preparation, dependable ingredients, and the details that make each bake feel special.

Rooted in community

From everyday loaves to celebration cakes, we want our bakery to feel welcoming to neighbors and visitors alike.
